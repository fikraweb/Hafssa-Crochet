## Boutique composition revision

The client found the first refinement too similar to the previous website. This revision intentionally changes the composition, rather than claiming that typographic adjustments constitute a new design. The existing dealt product/photo hero becomes a two-panel boutique composition: deep berry text panel, cream action, and a separate arched atelier photograph. On phones the copy and complete doll grouping occupy separate, legible areas. The original French copy, approved photographs, real contacts, two-column phone catalogue, drawer, checkout and dashboard remain intact.

The catalogue uses warm-white inset photograph frames, quieter square-soft corners, berry purchase buttons and a clearer separation between category navigation and products. This retains the client's rose/mauve branding while providing a visible structural change. No additional fonts, photographs or dependencies are introduced.

## Cinematic atelier hero

Client explicitly requested the earlier pale rose, full-bleed atelier composition, with independently moving and turning dolls. This request supersedes the previous berry/arch hero and the skill's default prohibition on continuous movement for a physical shop. The atelier is composited from an AI-edited empty plate of the existing illustrative workshop and a transparent AI-generated sheet of four complete marketing characters inspired by the approved scene. Original catalogue photographs are unchanged. Each character layer gently turns in perspective and shifts vertically on its own timing; this is 2.5D, not invented rear views or a true 360-degree model. A slow background camera move and pointer response add depth. Motion pauses outside the viewport, when the tab is hidden, under reduced-motion preference and through a compact accessible icon button. Brand colours, original French copy, product functions and other landing-page sections stay intact.
